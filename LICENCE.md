## 🧩 About

**Marauders Mod** is a comprehensive mod menu for Marauders featuring ESP, aim assist, loot radar, speed hack, no recoil, teleport, and more.

Based on popular mods like **Marauders Trainer**, **Marauders Cheat**, and **Marauders Hack**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Mods
- ESP — see players, loot, and extracts through walls
- Aim Assist — improved aiming with customizable FOV
- Loot Radar — highlight valuable loot within radius
- Speed Hack — adjust movement speed (1x to 5x)
- No Recoil — eliminate weapon recoil
- Teleport — instantly move to saved locations

### 🎨 Visuals & Interface
- Custom Crosshair — change crosshair style and color
- Player Info — display health, armor, and weapon
- Loot Filter — filter items by rarity and type
- Distance Indicators — show distance to targets

### 🏋️ Utility & Training
- Unlimited Stamina — no stamina drain
- No Fall Damage — survive any fall
- Instant Extract — speed up extraction timer
- Item Spawner — spawn items in raid
